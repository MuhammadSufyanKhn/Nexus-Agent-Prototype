# Business Policy Documents (PDF RAG)

This directory stores company policy documents used by the Policy Engine (`Nexus.Agent` & `Nexus.Tools`) for RAG retrieval and business rule enforcement.

## Included Policy Types
- `HR_Policy.pdf` (Onboarding rules, grade levels, default benefits)
- `IT_Policy.pdf` (Equipment allocation, developer role provisioning)
- `Expense_Policy.pdf` (Meal allowance caps, approval limits, receipt requirements)
- `Salary_Policy.pdf` (Compensation bands, developer salary ranges)
